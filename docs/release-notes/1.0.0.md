# Neon Work 1.0.0 — release notes

The first release of Neon Work, built from [Neon Deck](https://github.com/Yobzs/neon-deck-obsidian) 2.2.0 by Yobzs. It keeps Neon Deck's dashboard and look, and fits a vault you already have instead of asking the vault to fit the plugin. [FORK.md](../../FORK.md) lists every difference in detail.

## Install

Copy `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` into `<your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/neon-work/`, then enable **Neon Work** in *Settings → Community plugins*. To try it without touching your own notes, open the **sample vault** folder in this repository as a vault: the plugin is already installed and set up there.

## Safe with your notes

- **Read-only by default.** The plugin reads your vault and changes nothing until you switch read-only off; only its own settings files are written.
- **Nothing is moved, ever.** No `Core` folder: you choose a base folder for what the plugin makes for itself, and every location can be set on its own.

## Fits a vault organised by customer

- **Customers are the categories:** colours, card explorer chips and time tracking use your customer folders.
- **Projects:** notes tagged `project`, with your own status values in four groups, the customer from the `customer` property or folder, and `started`, `due`, `stage` and `note` shown. Status changes only update properties.
- **Tasks as in the Tasks plugin:** `#task` lines anywhere, grouped by 📅 due or ⏳ scheduled date, with a Tasks tab. Your Todo note is the Todo card; new tasks go at its head.
- **Daily and weekly notes** follow the core Daily notes settings, including formats with folders such as `YYYY/YYYY-ww`.
- **Research:** folders you choose (patterns such as `1.Customers/*/Research` gather every customer's), grouped by `status`.
- **New note** by customer and kind (note, meeting, person, project, research), each from its own template.
- **Claude card:** notes whose `claude` property asks for Claude's work, and notes Claude changed for you to review.
- **Inbox capture** and a **weekly review** from your own templates.

## New views

- **Timelines:** Mermaid Gantt charts of projects by customer and of dated tasks by note, with zoom and *Copy as Mermaid*.
- **Recently done** reads finished work from your notes (✅ dates on tasks, `completed` / `cancelled` on projects), so it works in read-only mode and counts work done anywhere in Obsidian.

## Trimmed

Tickets, PDF export, screenshots, Jira, quizzes and flashcards, the Write slash menu and selection toolbar, image moving, the "school" Revisit feature and "Fix properties" are gone. All settings are on one page with a section per part.
