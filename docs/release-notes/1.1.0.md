# Neon Work 1.1.0 — release notes

Neon Work now runs on phones and tablets. The desktop layout is unchanged.

## On phones and tablets

- **Dashboard, Tasks, Projects and Research** work on mobile. Time tracking, Write, the card explorer and the note graph stay desktop-only (their settings are kept, and they come back on the desktop).
- **Layouts:** one column on a phone held upright, two columns on a phone turned sideways or a tablet held upright, and the desktop grid on a tablet turned sideways. On a phone the cards come in the order you act on them: Quick capture, Tasks, Todo, Projects, Recently done, Claude, Activity, then the overview.
- **Touch:** a long press opens the same menu as a right-click (projects, research notes, pinned files); buttons are larger, and the edit and action buttons that appear on hover on the desktop are always shown.
- **Header:** the greeting and date, then one row of buttons that scrolls sideways.
- **Battery:** the background pattern stays still on mobile.

## Try the mobile layouts on the desktop

Open the developer console (Ctrl+Shift+I, or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac) and run `app.emulateMobile(true)`; Obsidian restarts as a phone. `app.emulateMobile(false)` goes back.
