# Neon Deck 2.0.2 — release notes

Released 5 Nov 2026. To update, replace `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` in `.obsidian/plugins/neon-deck/`.

## New
- **Drop files into Cards:** drag PDFs, DOCX, images or any file from your computer onto the Cards tab or the Cards sidebar. They are copied into the folder you are in, or into the folder card or row you drop them on. A name that is taken gets a number; nothing is overwritten. Files dragged from Obsidian's file list are moved there, with links updated.

## Better
- **Fits any width:** every Neon tab lays itself out by its own width, so a narrow sidebar or a small window works on any screen. Time stacks its panels, Tickets wraps its category tabs and rows, and dashboard card buttons move to their own row instead of sticking out.
- **Snappier switching:** Research sections and ticket categories change at once (before, a short wait or a slow fade-in).
