# Neon Deck 1.5.0 — release notes and check list

Released 3 Nov 2026. All four plugins (Neon Deck, Neon Tasks, Neon Tickets, Neon Time) are now 1.5.0. Everything was tested in real Obsidian. Time tests used demo data with saving switched off, and theme tests restored your theme afterwards.

Everything is still 100% local: no network calls, no web addresses, no remote fonts or code.

## Changed

**One main pane with sidebar nav**
- Dashboard, Cards, Tickets, Time and Graph no longer open as separate tabs. A nav list at the top of the left sidebar switches what the main pane shows, and the active view is highlighted.
- Notes, new ones included, open in the same pane, so nothing slides between tabs anymore. **Back / Forward** step through views and notes alike. Ctrl+click still opens a new tab.
- On first start, the old Neon tabs fold into one unpinned pane. Pinned tabs are always left alone, so a pinned tab makes notes open in new tabs again.

**Focus timer removed**
- Removed: the timer card, the status-bar timer, its commands and settings, the chime, the daily-note focus log, "focused today", the day streak, the focus tile in Totals and the Focus section of the weekly review.
- Old focus data stays unused in Neon Deck's saved data. Nothing was deleted.
- **Totals** took the timer's place in the left column: time worked large across the top, then days worked, tasks done, tickets done and notes. The tiles share the card's height and fit on both a desktop and a 2K laptop.

**Animations**
- First paint: the header drops in and cards rise in one by one. Vault pulse and Totals tiles rise in, their numbers count up and their sparklines draw themselves. Activity dots pop in week by week. The Time ring turns in, its segments draw one by one and the total counts up. Time rows rise in, also when you switch days in the Time tab.
- While you work: numbers count again when they change, and a new time entry slides in with a short colour flash. Today's Activity dot and the live-time dot breathe.
- Hover: cards get a soft accent edge and glow with a tilt of their icon, and tiles, Time rows and the Time ring respond too.
- Redraws never replay the opening motion. With *Reduce motion* on, nothing moves and numbers show at once.

**Graph**
- New nodes that follow the theme: a dark core lit by the cluster colour, a crisp ring and a soft glow (no glow in light themes). Hubs get a faint outer orbit.
- Links are thinner and fade on dense graphs. Labels use the theme font, bright on hubs and muted on small nodes.
- Hover and selection show an accent ring with a slow ripple.

## Fixes

1. **Duplicate tabs after a restart:** each restart of Neon Deck added another Card explorer tab in the left sidebar and another Dashboard. Tabs are now arranged once Obsidian has put the old ones back, and any copies are closed.
