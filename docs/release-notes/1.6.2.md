# Neon Deck 1.6.2 — release notes

Released 3 Nov 2026. All five plugins are now 1.6.2. Tested in real Obsidian with temporary tickets and templates, which were removed afterwards.

## New: ticket templates (Neon Tickets)

- **Templates tab** in the Tickets screen, next to Active / Waiting / Done / All. Add, edit, duplicate and delete templates. Clicking a template edits it, and the filter box searches names and steps.
- **Template editor:** a name, steps (edit in place, move up or down, remove; Enter adds the next), and optionally a description, priority and "due in N days".
- **New ticket form:** a Template row fills in the template's steps, plus its description (only when empty), priority and due date. Your own steps and text stay. Switching templates replaces only what the previous one filled in, and None puts the priority and due date back. **Use** on a template opens the form with it already picked.
- **Local:** templates live in Neon Tickets' own saved data on this PC. They're never notes, so they don't show up in Cards, the graph or search.

## Changed

- **Adding steps is instant:** a step shows the moment you press Enter (about 3ms, was about 260ms). The box stays open and saves run in the background, in order.
- **The log keeps only finished steps:** adding a step no longer writes a log line. Checking one logs "Step done", and unchecking stays quiet.

## Fixes (template feature)

- Picking a template no longer wipes steps you typed yourself.
- "None" restores the priority and due date from before.
- Broken saved templates are repaired or skipped instead of breaking the tab.
- Removing a step with × always removes that row, even when two steps share a name.
- Very long template names are cut off instead of stretching the layout.
