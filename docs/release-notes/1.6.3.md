# Neon Deck 1.6.3 — release notes

Released 3 Nov 2026. All five plugins are now 1.6.3. Tested in real Obsidian with temporary tickets, images and notes, which were removed afterwards.

## New

**Screenshots on tickets (Neon Tickets)**
- A **Screenshots** section on every ticket, in the tab and the pop-up. Add images by pasting (Ctrl+V while the ticket is in front), by dropping image files on it, or with **+**.
- Images are saved straight into `Core/Images/Tickets/`, named after the ticket ID or title plus the time, linked in the ticket and noted in the log.
- Thumbnails open full size. **×** removes a screenshot, and the image goes to the system trash.

**Export a ticket as PDF**
- The export button on a ticket (top right) builds a report: a details table (ID, Jira, status, priority, opened / due / completed, time logged), the description, every step with when it was done, all screenshots, time entries and the log.
- The report opens and Obsidian's own **Export to PDF** asks where to save it. A copy stays in `Core/Tickets/Exports/` and is updated on the next export.

**Styled tables (Neon Write)**
- Tables in notes get a rounded card, a header tinted in the theme colour, quiet lines and alternating rows, in editing and reading mode. Wide tables scroll sideways inside the note. There's a "Styled tables" switch in Neon Write's settings.

## Fixes (from the test pass)

- Exporting from the pop-up now closes it first, so the report and PDF dialog aren't hidden behind it.
- Exporting again reuses the report's tab instead of opening another one.
- Reports are marked untracked, so reading them never logs time.
- A screenshot file renamed or deleted outside the ticket now shows up right away (it follows a rename, or shows "Missing").
- Wide tables no longer run past the right edge of the note.
