# Holidays

Working days, holidays and time off for Planning. This is the shared note: every project uses it unless its customer's folder has its own `Holidays` note (a customer in another country, say). Planning skips these days in its dates, shades them on the Gantt chart, and a task takes longer when its owner is off.

## All

For everyone: the public holidays (worked out each year), the working week, and any days the whole team is off, with a name.

- public holidays: England and Wales
- working days: Mon-Fri
- 2026-12-29 to 2026-12-31 Office closed
